What you can hire me for

Consulting work is shaped around the problem, the timeline, and the amount of hands-on delivery needed. If you are not sure which structure fits, say so — I will tell you what the best engagement shape looks like.

Information systems consulting

Operations and architecture support

  • Systems design and operational planning — structure work, define workflows, and keep technical decisions aligned to real operating needs.
  • Security-minded architecture review — assess cloud, network, and control design with practical resilience and governance in mind.
  • Process, documentation, and governance — improve handoffs, review cycles, and operational readiness without adding unnecessary overhead.
  • Project strategy and delivery support — clarify scope, set milestones, and help teams move from draft plans to working execution.

Work can be direct consulting, short-term engagement, or support to a project team already in motion.

Project delivery

Builds, automation, and public code

  • Focused engineering builds — Python, automation, tooling, and small software systems that solve a real operational need.
  • Cloud and network review — design feedback and implementation support across platform and service choices.
  • Public code and portfolio work — tools, scripts, and project artifacts that can be reviewed and reused beyond a single engagement.
  • Hands-on second opinion — review an approach, architecture, or delivery plan and tell you where the risk or friction is likely to show up.

Fixed fee when the boundary is clear. Hourly when it is not. No sales funnel or sponsorship path.

Also in the toolkit

Not the lead offer, but it shows up when the job needs operational depth: enterprise LAN/WAN, fiber and structured cabling, SATCOM and microwave, 24/7 fault isolation, and hands-on systems coordination when something must keep working. The background is technical and operational, not just advisory.

What I will not take

  • Unauthorized testing or review of systems you do not own or cannot authorize in writing.
  • Work that needs a registered company or set-aside I cannot honestly claim.
  • Retainer-only advisory with no concrete deliverables.
  • Anything that belongs in a full law-enforcement or formal incident-response process — I can triage, then hand off.

How to buy

Direct

Email or WhatsApp. I invoice as an independent consultant. Suitable for teams that want direct access to a senior operator and builder.

Project support

If you need a specialist embedded for a portion of delivery, say so early. The work can be scoped, time-boxed, or delivered in a fixed set of milestones.

Short notice

If the need is this week, put that in the subject. I will say yes or no quickly rather than schedule a discovery call for its own sake.
